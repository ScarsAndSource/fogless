Fogless Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Fogless is a personal expense tracker. You type what you spent or received; the app parses, categorises and stores it in your private ledger. Fogless does not connect to your bank, read your SMS, or access your contacts. It only knows what you type or say to it.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (the password is handled by our authentication provider and is stored only in hashed form; we never see or store it in plain text), and your device's time zone.
  • Your ledger: the transactions, notes, categories, payment methods, balances, shared-money entries (including any names of people you type), learned shortcuts, and the chat history of messages you send to the app.
  • Voice notes: if you use the microphone button, the recording is sent to be transcribed and then discarded by us. Only the resulting text is kept in your chat history.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (such as IP address and time of request) kept by our hosting providers for security and reliability.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell your data.

How we use it

Only to run the app for you: to sign you in, to understand and record what you type, to show your balances and history, and to protect the service from abuse. Your ledger is visible only to your account.

Who processes it

  • Supabase — authentication and database storage.
  • Vercel — hosting of the app and its server.
  • Groq — the text of messages you send (and voice recordings) is sent to Groq's AI service to be parsed or transcribed. Do not type anything into the app that you would not want processed this way.
  • Google Fonts — the app loads its fonts from Google, which means Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

Cookies

Two strictly necessary, HttpOnly session cookies keep you signed in. There are no tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. You can erase your whole ledger with /reset confirm, or delete your account and all of its data from the app (Quick Actions → Delete Account). Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a limited time after deletion.

Your choices and rights

  • Access / export: download your data any time as CSV or JSON from Quick Actions.
  • Correction: edit or delete any entry in the app.
  • Deletion: delete your account in the app. If you cannot sign in, email us at the address below from your registered email address and we will delete the account and its data.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have further rights, including to withdraw consent and to raise a grievance. Contact us below.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and each account's data is isolated from every other account. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Fogless is not intended for anyone under 18.
